About AgroKuching

AgroKuching is a platform connecting farmers, wholesalers, and buyers in Kuching, Sarawak. Our mission is to make agricultural trade easier, more transparent, and more accessible for everyone in the farming community.

We provide a marketplace for agricultural products, real-time market price updates, and direct messaging between buyers and sellers.
